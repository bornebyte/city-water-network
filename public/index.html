    Findings from the report

    • Original network is a wheel (5-cycle plus hub): 6 vertices, 10 edges, planar with 6 faces, chromatic number 4, P(G,4) = 120.
    • Removing WE keeps it connected and planar and lowers the chromatic number to 3, with 6 valid 3-colourings.
    • Limits: no distances, flow, pressure, pipe size or cost data. This is a planning model, not a hydraulic design.
